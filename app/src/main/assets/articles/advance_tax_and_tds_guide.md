# Advance Tax Due Dates, FD TDS Thresholds & Form 15G/15H

Salaried employees rely on employer TDS, but the moment you earn **FD interest, capital gains, freelance fees, or rent**, you may owe **Advance Tax**—and missing deadlines triggers mandatory interest under Sections 234B and 234C.

## Who Must Pay Advance Tax?

If your estimated total income tax liability for the year—**after subtracting TDS/TCS**—is **₹10,000 or more**, you must pay Advance Tax in four instalments:

| Due Date | Minimum Cumulative Tax Payable |
| --- | --- |
| **15 June** | 15% of annual tax |
| **15 September** | 45% of annual tax |
| **15 December** | 75% of annual tax |
| **15 March** | 100% of annual tax |

- **Senior Citizens Exemption:** Resident senior citizens (age 60+) with **no business or professional income** are **exempt** from paying Advance Tax (they can pay Self-Assessment Tax before filing ITR).

## Bank FD TDS Thresholds & Form 15G / 15H

- Banks deduct **10% TDS** (or **20%** if PAN is not linked/inoperative) when your aggregate FD/RD interest across all branches of a bank exceeds the notified annual threshold (higher threshold for senior citizens—check LoanJB's **Data & Rates** or **FD Calculator** for current FY limits).
- **TDS Is Not Final Tax:** If you fall in the 30% slab, you must pay the remaining 20% difference yourself as Advance Tax; if you are in the 0% bracket, claim a refund in ITR or submit **Form 15G** (below 60) / **Form 15H** (60+) at the start of every April.

> Check your **AIS (Annual Information Statement)** and **Form 26AS** every quarter so no bank interest or dividend gets missed before March 15.
