# CIBIL & Credit Score Bands: What 750+ Unlocks

In India, four RBI-licensed Credit Information Companies—**TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, and CRIF High Mark**—track your repayment history on a 300–900 scale. Your score directly determines both loan approval and the **Credit Risk Premium** added to your interest rate.

## Indian Credit Score Bands

| Score Range | Lender Assessment | Typical Impact on Loan Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **780 – 900** | Excellent | Best carded interest rate, lowest spread, fastest approval |
| **750 – 779** | Very Good | Smooth approval at standard bank rates |
| **700 – 749** | Fair / Moderate | Approved with +0.25% to +0.75% higher spread |
| **650 – 699** | Below Average | Higher rate, lower LTV, or guarantor required |
| **Below 650** | High Risk | High rejection risk for unsecured loans & cards |

## Why a 0.50% Rate Discount Matters

On a **₹50 Lakh home loan for 20 years**, a borrower with a 785 score getting **8.40%** pays roughly **₹3.4 Lakh less in total interest** than a borrower with a 710 score priced at **8.90%**.

## Know Your Free Annual Entitlement

- Every Indian resident is entitled to **at least one free Full Credit Report (FFCR)** per calendar year from each of the 4 bureaus under RBI guidelines.
- Checking your own score directly via a bureau or authorized portal is a **soft inquiry** and **never reduces your score**.

> Under RBI's updated reporting rules, lenders must update borrower credit records with bureaus **every 15 days** (fortnightly), meaning improvements—and missed payments—show up much faster than before.
