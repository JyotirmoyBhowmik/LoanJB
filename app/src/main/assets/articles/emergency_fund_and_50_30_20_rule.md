# Emergency Fund & 50/30/20 Budget Blueprint

Before starting aggressive equity SIPs or prepaying a home loan, every household needs two structural foundations: a **cash-flow budget** and a **ring-fenced Emergency Fund**.

## The 50 / 30 / 20 Budgeting Framework

Split your monthly **net take-home income** into three clear buckets (customize splits in LoanJB's **Financial Rules** planner):

- **50% — Essential Needs:** Rent/Home EMI, groceries, utilities, school fees, transport, insurance premiums.
- **30% — Wants & Lifestyle:** Dining out, travel, gadgets, subscriptions, shopping.
- **20% (or more!) — Savings & Debt Freedom:** SIPs, EPF/PPF, emergency reserve, extra loan prepayments.

If your essential EMIs alone consume 45% of income, tighten the Wants bucket first so savings never fall to zero.

## Sizing & Parking Your Emergency Fund

| Household Profile | Recommended Emergency Buffer |
| --- | --- |
| Dual-income salaried household | **6 months** of essential expenses + EMIs |
| Single-income salaried household | **6 – 9 months** of essential expenses + EMIs |
| Self-employed / commission / variable income | **9 – 12 months** of essential expenses + EMIs |

### Where to Keep It (Liquidity Beats Yield)

1. **Tier 1 (Instant Access — 30%):** High-liquidity Savings Account or Auto-Sweep FD.
2. **Tier 2 (1-Day Access — 70%):** Split between **Sweep-in / Short-Term Bank FDs** and **Overnight / Liquid Mutual Funds** (look for instant-redemption facilities up to ₹50,000/day per folio).
3. **Never park emergency cash in equity mutual funds or stocks**—market crashes often coincide with job losses!
