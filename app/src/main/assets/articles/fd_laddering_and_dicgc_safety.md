# FD Laddering & DICGC ₹5 Lakh Deposit Insurance Explained

Putting all your savings into a single 5-year Fixed Deposit exposes you to **reinvestment risk**, **premature withdrawal penalties**, and **bank concentration risk**. Two simple safeguards solve all three: **FD Laddering** and **DICGC limits**.

## What Is FD Laddering?

Instead of locking ₹5,00,000 into one 5-year FD, divide it into five equal ₹1,00,000 FDs maturing in **1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 years**:

1. Every single year, one FD matures naturally—giving you **penalty-free liquidity** if needed.
2. If you do not need the cash, reinvest the maturing FD for a fresh 5-year term at the top of the ladder.
3. You automatically capture rising interest rate cycles without breaking existing FDs early.

## DICGC ₹5 Lakh Deposit Insurance Rules

The **Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC)**—a wholly owned subsidiary of RBI—insures deposits at every scheduled commercial bank, small finance bank, and eligible co-operative bank:

- **Coverage Limit:** Up to **₹5,00,000 per depositor, per bank**—including **both principal AND accrued interest** across all Savings, Current, FD, and RD accounts held in the *same right and capacity* across all branches of that bank.
- **Different Capacities Count Separately:** An account in your sole name (`A`) and a joint account with a different primary holder (`B + A`) are insured separately.

## Smart Safety Habit for High-Yield FDs

> When chasing higher FD rates at smaller or Small Finance Banks, cap total principal + expected maturity interest at **₹4.5 Lakh per depositor per bank** so you remain comfortably inside the ₹5 Lakh DICGC umbrella.
