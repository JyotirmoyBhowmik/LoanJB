# The 5 Factors That Drive Your Credit Score

While exact scoring algorithms are proprietary, credit bureaus across India weigh five core pillars of borrower behaviour. Mastering the top two handles **nearly two-thirds** of your score.

## Approximate Weightage of Score Pillars

1. **Payment History (~35%):** Paying every EMI and credit card bill on or before the due date. Even a single **30+ Days Past Due (DPD)** mark can drop a 780 score by 60–90 points and stay on your report for years.
2. **Credit Utilization Ratio (~30%):** How much of your revolving credit card limit you use each statement cycle. Keeping utilization **below 30%** (ideally 10%–20%) signals healthy liquidity.
3. **Credit History Age (~15%):** Average age of your active accounts. Avoid closing your **oldest lifetime-free credit card**, as it anchors your credit history length.
4. **Credit Mix (~10%):** A balanced mix of **secured credit** (home/auto loan) and **unsecured credit** (credit cards/personal loans) scores better than heavy reliance on unsecured debt alone.
5. **New Credit & Hard Inquiries (~10%):** Multiple loan or card applications in a short window trigger "credit-hungry" risk flags.

## The "Minimum Amount Due" Trap

Paying only the **Minimum Amount Due (MAD)** on a credit card keeps your account from being marked overdue, *but*:

- You lose the interest-free grace period immediately.
- Finance charges of **36%–44% p.a. + 18% GST** apply on the unpaid balance and new purchases.
- Persistently high revolving balances push utilization above 30% and steadily drag your score down.

> Always set **Auto-Debit for Total Amount Due** on credit cards and maintain a 1-EMI buffer in your loan-linked savings account.
