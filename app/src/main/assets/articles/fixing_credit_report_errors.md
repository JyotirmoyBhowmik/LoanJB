# How to Dispute Errors & Rebuild a Low Credit Score

A clerical error on your credit report—a closed loan still showing active, a duplicate account, or a wrong overdue flag—can cost you lakhs in extra interest. RBI provides strict timelines to fix errors, plus proven steps to rebuild after past defaults.

## Step 1: Audit Your Report for Common Errors

Download your free report and verify:

- **Account Status:** Closed loans must show `Closed`, not `Active`, `Settled`, or `Written Off`.
- **DPD Grid:** Confirm `000` (zero days past due) for months you paid on time.
- **Unknown Accounts / Inquiries:** Flag any unrecognized loan immediately as potential identity fraud.
- **Personal Details:** Check PAN, date of birth, and address spelling.

## Step 2: File a Bureau Dispute (RBI 30-Day Compensation Rule)

1. Raise an online dispute directly on the bureau portal (CIBIL / Experian / Equifax / CRIF) citing the **Control Number / Report Number** and uploading your Bank NOC or closure statement.
2. Simultaneously email the lender's nodal officer.
3. Under RBI's credit information framework, if a confirmed dispute is not resolved within **30 calendar days**, the complainant is entitled to **₹100 per day of delay** in compensation.

## Step 3: Rebuilding From Below 680

- **Never "Settle" if you can pay in full:** A `"Settled"` tag hurts your score for up to 7 years; if you settled earlier, ask the bank to pay the waived difference and update status to `"Closed"`.
- **Use a Secured FD-Backed Credit Card:** Open a small FD-backed card, spend 10%–15% of the limit monthly, and pay in full for 6–12 consecutive months.
- **Pause fresh unsecured applications** until your score crosses 740+.
