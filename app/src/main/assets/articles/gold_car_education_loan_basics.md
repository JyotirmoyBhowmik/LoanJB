# Gold, Vehicle & Education Loans: Key Rules Compared

Not all retail loans in India follow home loan rules. Collateral, moratorium periods, and repayment structures differ sharply across gold, vehicle, and education loans.

## 1. Gold Loans (Short-Term Secured Liquidity)

- **LTV Cap:** RBI caps gold loan LTV at **75%** of the value of gold jewellery (22K purity adjusted; stones/making charges excluded).
- **Repayment Modes:** Standard EMI, **interest-only monthly + bullet principal** at maturity, or overdraft facility.
- **Auction Risk:** If gold prices fall sharply or interest remains unpaid past maturity, LTV breaches 75% and lenders can issue an auction notice. Always track accrued interest on bullet loans.

## 2. Vehicle Loans (Car, EV & Two-Wheeler)

- **Hypothecation:** The vehicle RC carries the lender's hypothecation until closure.
- **EV Discounts:** Several Indian banks offer **0.10%–0.25% lower interest rates** on Green / Electric Vehicle loans.
- **Depreciation Trap:** Avoid 7-year car loans if possible—cars depreciate quickly, and a 3–5 year tenure saves substantial interest.

## 3. Education Loans (Study in India & Abroad)

- **Moratorium Period:** Course duration + 6 to 12 months grace period before full EMI starts.
- **Simple Interest During Moratorium:** Paying *simple interest* during the study period prevents unpaid interest from being capitalized onto your principal—and qualifies for **Section 80E** tax deduction under the Old Regime.
- **Collateral Thresholds:** Under IBA Model Education Loan guidelines, loans up to ₹7.5 lakh are typically available without third-party collateral (often backed by CGFSEL guarantee).

> Match the loan product to the asset life: never fund short-term consumption with long-term debt, and always check moratorium capitalization before signing an education loan.
