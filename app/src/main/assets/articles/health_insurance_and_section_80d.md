# Family Floater Health Insurance, Super Top-Ups & 80D

Medical inflation in Indian urban hospitals runs at **10%–14% per year**—far faster than general CPI inflation. A single ICU hospitalization can easily wipe out years of SIP savings unless you hold adequate personal health cover.

## Don't Rely Solely on Employer Group Health Cover

- Employer cover disappears the day you switch jobs, take a sabbatical, start a business, or retire—often at an age when new health conditions make fresh personal policies expensive or declined.
- Maintain a **personal Family Floater** (₹10–₹15 Lakh in metros; ₹5–₹10 Lakh in Tier-2/3 cities) alongside your employer plan.

## 5 Clauses to Inspect Before Buying

1. **No Room Rent Sub-Limits:** Room rent caps (e.g., 1% of Sum Insured) trigger **proportionate deductions** on doctor fees, surgery charges, and diagnostics—always pick **Single Private AC Room or No Cap**.
2. **Zero Co-Payment:** Avoid mandatory 10%–20% co-pay clauses for younger families.
3. **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** Standard IRDAI waiting period is up to 3 years; buying early while healthy lets you finish waiting periods smoothly.
4. **Restoration Benefit & No-Claim Bonus (NCB):** Check whether NCB protects against inflation without shrinking after a single small claim.
5. **Super Top-Up Policy:** Pair a ₹10 Lakh base policy with a **₹25–₹50 Lakh Super Top-Up** (with a ₹10 Lakh deductible) to get massive catastrophic cover at a fraction of base-policy premiums!

## Section 80D Tax Deduction (Old Tax Regime)

- Up to **₹25,000** for self, spouse, and children (₹50,000 if senior citizen).
- Additional **₹25,000** for parents below 60, or **₹50,000** for senior citizen parents (60+).
- Includes up to **₹5,000** for preventive health check-ups within the overall limit.
