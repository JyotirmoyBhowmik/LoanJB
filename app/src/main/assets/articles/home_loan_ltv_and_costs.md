# Home Loan LTV Caps, Stamp Duty & Hidden Fees in India

When budgeting for a home purchase, the headline property price is only part of the upfront cash you need. RBI's **Loan-to-Value (LTV)** limits and ancillary charges significantly affect your down payment.

## RBI Loan-to-Value (LTV) Slabs

RBI caps the maximum percentage of a property's agreement value that banks and housing finance companies can finance:

| Loan Amount Slab | Maximum LTV Allowed | Minimum Own Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ₹30 Lakh | Up to 90% | 10% |
| Above ₹30 Lakh to ₹75 Lakh | Up to 80% | 20% |
| Above ₹75 Lakh | Up to 75% | 25% |

- **Stamp duty and registration** (typically 5%–8% of property value depending on the state) are generally **excluded** from the LTV base for loans above ₹10 lakh.
- Plan for **25%–30% total upfront cash** (down payment + stamp duty + registration + moving/interiors).

## Ancillary Loan Charges to Watch

1. **Processing Fee + 18% GST:** Usually 0.25%–0.50% of loan amount, often capped at ₹10,000–₹25,000 during festive offers.
2. **Legal & Technical Valuation Fee:** ₹3,000–₹10,000 paid to empaneled advocates and valuers.
3. **MODT / Equitable Mortgage Registration:** 0.1%–0.5% of the loan amount in many Indian states (e.g., Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu).
4. **CERSAI Registration Fee:** Nominal statutory fee (~₹100 + GST).
5. **Optional Loan Protection Insurance:** Never let a lender bundle a single-premium loan insurance policy without comparing a standalone pure term cover.

> Always compare total **5-year or full-tenure outflow including fees and GST**, not just the advertised starting interest rate.
