# Home Loan Tax Benefits: Section 24(b), 80C & Joint Loans

Home loan deductions can significantly reduce your effective borrowing cost—**provided you opt for the Old Tax Regime** (or own a **let-out** property under applicable rules).

## 1. Interest Deduction — Section 24(b)

- **Self-Occupied Property (Old Regime):** Deduct up to **₹2,00,000 per financial year** on home loan interest (provided construction finishes within 5 years from the end of the FY in which the loan was taken; otherwise capped at ₹30,000).
- **Let-Out / Rented Property:** Interest paid can be set off against rental income; overall loss from house property set-off against other heads is capped at **₹2,00,000** per year in the Old Regime (excess carried forward up to 8 assessment years).
- **New Tax Regime:** Self-occupied Section 24(b) deduction is **not available**.

## 2. Principal Repayment — Section 80C (Old Regime Only)

- Principal repaid + stamp duty & registration charges (in the year of purchase) qualify within the shared **₹1,50,000 Section 80C limit** (shared with EPF, PPF, ELSS, LIC, tuition fees).
- **5-Year Lock-In Warning:** If you sell the house within **5 years** from the end of the FY in which possession was taken, earlier Section 80C deductions are reversed and added back to taxable income.

## 3. Joint Home Loan Advantage

When spouses or family members are **both co-owners AND co-borrowers** contributing to EMIs:

- **Each co-borrower** can independently claim up to **₹2 Lakh under Section 24(b)** and **₹1.5 Lakh under Section 80C** in the Old Regime—proportionate to ownership share!

> Collect the **Provisional Interest Certificate** in January for employer TDS planning and the **Final Interest Certificate** after March 31 for ITR filing.
