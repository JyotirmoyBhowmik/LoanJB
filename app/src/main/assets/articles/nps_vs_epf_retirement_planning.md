# EPF, NPS & Inflation-Adjusted Retirement Planning

Unlike education or buying a car, **nobody gives you a loan for retirement**. With rising Indian life expectancy (85+ years) and 6%–7% long-term household inflation, planning retirement purely on fixed deposits often leaves a severe late-life shortfall.

## Why Inflation Doubles Expenses Every ~12 Years

At **6% annual inflation**, a household spending **₹60,000/month** today will need:
- **~₹1,07,000/month** in 10 years
- **~₹1,92,000/month** in 20 years
- **~₹2,58,000/month** in 25 years—just to maintain the *exact same lifestyle*!

## EPF vs NPS: Complementary Pillars

| Feature | EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) | NPS (National Pension System — Tier I) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asset Class | 100% Fixed-Income (govt-notified rate) | Market-linked Equity (up to 75%) + Corporate/G-Sec Debt |
| Tax Status | EEE (up to ₹2.5L/yr employee contribution cap) | 60% lump sum tax-free at 60; 40% annuity pension taxable |
| Employer Tax Edge | Employer 12% exempt within ₹7.5L combined cap | **Sec 80CCD(2)** allowed in **BOTH New & Old Regimes**! |
| Lock-In | Till retirement / specific withdrawals | Till age 60 (partial withdrawals for specified needs) |

## Three-Step Retirement Roadmap

1. **Calculate your real target corpus** in LoanJB's **Retirement Planner** using realistic inflation (6%–7%) and post-retirement returns (7%–8%).
2. **Use EPF + PPF as your stable debt anchor** and **NPS + diversified equity index/flexi-cap SIPs** for inflation-beating growth.
3. **De-risk gradually 3–5 years before retiring** by shifting upcoming expenses into safer debt instruments so a sudden market fall never derails your retirement date.
