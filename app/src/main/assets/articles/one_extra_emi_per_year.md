# The 1-Extra-EMI Rule: Cutting Years Off a 20-Year Loan

You do not need a huge windfall to become debt-free years ahead of schedule. Simply paying **one extra EMI every year**—or stepping up your EMI by **5% annually** as your salary grows—transforms a 20-year home loan.

## Why One Extra EMI Works So Well

Because your regular 12 EMIs already cover all monthly interest due, a 13th payment goes **100% toward principal reduction**. That eliminated principal stops accruing interest for the rest of the loan's life.

| Strategy (₹50L Loan @ 8.50%, 20 Yrs) | Effective Tenure | Total Interest Saved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular EMI Only (₹43,391/mo) | 20 yrs 0 mos | ₹0 (Baseline) |
| **+1 Extra EMI Every Year** | ~16 yrs 6 mos | **~₹11.2 Lakh** |
| **+5% Annual EMI Step-Up** | ~12 yrs 4 mos | **~₹22.8 Lakh** |

## Easy Ways to Automate It

1. **Direct annual bonuses or tax refunds** toward principal prepayment.
2. **Use a Home Loan Overdraft (MaxGain / Home Saver)** if your bank offers one at a minimal rate premium: surplus cash parked in the linked account lowers daily interest while staying liquid.
3. **Set a target loan-free date** using LoanJB's **Part-Payment Planner** to see the exact extra monthly amount required.

> Even ₹3,500 extra per month from Month 1 on a ₹50 Lakh loan cuts over 3 years off your tenure without straining monthly budgets.
