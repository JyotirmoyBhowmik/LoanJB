# PPF Complete Guide: 5th-of-Month Rule & 5-Year Extensions

The **Public Provident Fund (PPF)** remains India's gold standard for sovereign-backed, **EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)** long-term debt wealth creation. Small operational habits can add lakhs to your 15–25 year maturity corpus.

## Core PPF Rules at a Glance

- **Tenure:** 15 full financial years (from the end of the FY in which the account is opened).
- **Annual Deposit Cap:** Minimum ₹500 to maximum **₹1,50,000 per financial year** (combined across your own and minor children's accounts).
- **Interest Rate:** Government-notified **quarterly**; compounded annually on March 31.
- **Tax Status:** Contributions qualify under Section 80C (Old Regime); **interest and maturity proceeds are 100% tax-free in BOTH New and Old Tax Regimes**.

## The "Before the 5th of Every Month" Rule

PPF interest is calculated each month on the **lowest balance between the close of the 5th day and the last day of that month**.

- Depositing on **April 1–5** earns interest for **all 12 months** of the financial year.
- Depositing on **April 6** loses an entire month's interest for April!
- Similarly, if investing monthly, schedule deposits on the **1st–4th** of every month.

## Extending PPF in 5-Year Blocks After Maturity

After 15 years, you do not have to close your PPF account:

1. **Extend WITH Fresh Contributions (submit Form 4 within 1 year of maturity):** Keep depositing up to ₹1.5L/year and earning tax-free compounding on your giant accumulated balance (1 partial withdrawal up to 60% of block-start balance allowed).
2. **Extend WITHOUT Contributions (automatic default):** Existing corpus continues earning tax-free interest with 1 free withdrawal per year of any amount.
