# RBI Foreclosure Rules, Zero Penalty & RC/Property NOC

Closing a loan does not end with paying the last rupee—you must ensure zero unlawful penalties are charged and collect every **original collateral and No Objection Certificate (NOC)** on time.

## Zero Foreclosure Penalty on Floating-Rate Loans

- Under RBI directives, banks and NBFCs **cannot charge any foreclosure or prepayment penalty** on floating-rate term loans sanctioned to **individual borrowers** (for non-business purposes).
- Fixed-rate loans, business/commercial loans, and corporate borrowings may carry foreclosure charges as disclosed in the Key Fact Statement.

## RBI 30-Day Original Document Return Mandate

RBI's Responsible Lending Conduct directions protect borrowers after full loan settlement:

- Lenders **must release all original movable/immovable property documents** and remove charges registered with any registry within **30 days** of full repayment.
- If a bank delays returning original property papers beyond 30 days due to its own fault, RBI mandates compensation of **₹5,000 per day of delay** to the borrower.

## Mandatory Loan Closure Checklist

1. **Loan Closure / No-Dues Certificate (NDC)** stating zero outstanding balance.
2. **Vehicle Loans:** Collect **2 signed copies of Form 35** plus the Bank NOC and apply for **Hypothecation Termination** on the **Parivahan** portal to get a clean RC.
3. **Home Loans:** Collect original sale deed, chain documents, and file **MODT / Charge Release** at the Sub-Registrar office + verify CERSAI removal.
4. **Credit Bureau Check:** Verify after 45–60 days that the account shows **"Closed"** (not "Settled") on CIBIL/Experian.
