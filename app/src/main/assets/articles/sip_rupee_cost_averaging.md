# How SIP Works: Rupee Cost Averaging & Compounding

A **Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)** invests a fixed rupee amount into a mutual fund on a chosen date every month. Its real power is behavioural and mathematical: **discipline plus Rupee Cost Averaging**.

## How Rupee Cost Averaging Protects You

When markets are high, your fixed ₹10,000 buys **fewer units**. When markets dip 15%, the same ₹10,000 automatically buys **more units** at a lower NAV—lowering your average cost per unit without trying to time market bottoms.

| Month | SIP Amount | Fund NAV | Units Bought |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Month 1 | ₹10,000 | ₹100 | 100.00 |
| Month 2 (Dip) | ₹10,000 | ₹80 | 125.00 |
| Month 3 (Rebound) | ₹10,000 | ₹100 | 100.00 |
| **Total** | **₹30,000** | Avg Cost: **₹92.31** | **325.00 units** (Value: ₹32,500) |

Notice that even though the NAV finished right back where it started (₹100), your SIP made a **+8.33% profit** simply by continuing during the dip!

## Why Time Beats Timing

Compounding is back-loaded:

- Investing **₹10,000/month at 12% p.a.** builds ~₹23.2 Lakh in **10 years**, ~₹50.5 Lakh in **15 years**, and **~₹99.9 Lakh in 20 years**.
- Nearly half the 20-year wealth is generated in the **final 5 years alone**.

> *Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Returns are not guaranteed, and past performance does not indicate future results. Never stop equity SIPs during market corrections if your goal is 7+ years away.*
