# Step-Up SIP: Why a 10% Annual Hike Multiplies Corpus

Most investors start an SIP early in their career and leave the monthly amount frozen for a decade—even as their salary grows 8%–12% every year. Adding an annual **Step-Up (Top-Up) SIP** bridges the gap between modest starting budgets and multi-crore goals.

## Flat SIP vs 10% Step-Up SIP (15 Years @ 12% Assumed Return)

Suppose you start with **₹10,000/month** today:

| Strategy | Year 1 Monthly | Year 15 Monthly | Total Invested | Indicative Corpus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flat SIP (0% Step-Up) | ₹10,000 | ₹10,000 | ₹18.0 Lakh | ~₹50.5 Lakh |
| **5% Annual Step-Up** | ₹10,000 | ₹19,799 | ₹25.9 Lakh | ~₹67.1 Lakh |
| **10% Annual Step-Up** | ₹10,000 | ₹37,975 | ₹38.1 Lakh | **~₹90.4 Lakh** |

A 10% annual step-up grows your indicative corpus by **nearly 80%** over 15 years—while keeping Year 1 completely affordable!

## Always Adjust Long-Term Targets for Inflation

- At **6% annual inflation**, a ₹50 Lakh goal today (such as higher education or retirement expenses) will cost **~₹1.20 Crore in 15 years** and **~₹1.60 Crore in 20 years**.
- Pairing a Step-Up SIP with an inflation-adjusted goal target prevents painful shortfalls later.

## Practical Step-Up Habit

1. Schedule your SIP step-up in **April or May** right after your annual appraisal—before lifestyle inflation absorbs the raise.
