# Sukanya Samriddhi (SSY) & Senior Citizens' Savings (SCSS)

Among Government of India Small Savings Schemes, **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)** and **Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme (SCSS)** consistently offer the highest sovereign interest rates—each tailored to a distinct life stage.

## Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) — For a Girl Child

- **Eligibility:** Girl child below **age 10** (maximum 2 accounts per family; 3 in case of twins/triplets).
- **Deposit Window:** Contribute for **15 years** (₹250 to ₹1,50,000 per FY); account matures after **21 years** from opening (or upon marriage after age 18).
- **Partial Withdrawal:** Up to **50% of previous FY closing balance** allowed once the girl turns 18 (or passes Class 10) for higher education.
- **Tax Benefit:** Full **EEE**—interest and maturity are 100% tax-free under both tax regimes.

## Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme (SCSS) — For Retirees (Age 60+)

- **Eligibility:** Age 60+ (or 55–60 for VRS/superannuation retirees; 50+ for retired defence personnel, subject to scheme conditions).
- **Maximum Investment Cap:** Up to **₹30 Lakh** per eligible individual.
- **Interest Payout:** Credited **quarterly** (April 1, July 1, October 1, January 1) directly into your linked savings account—ideal for pension cash flow.
- **Tenure:** 5 years, extendable in **3-year blocks**.
- **Taxation:** Interest is **taxable at slab rate** (TDS applies above the senior citizen threshold unless Form 15H is submitted).

> Unlike market-linked funds, **SSY and SCSS carry sovereign Government of India backing**, though notified rates are reviewed by the Ministry of Finance **every quarter**.
