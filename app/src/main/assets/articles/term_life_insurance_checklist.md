# Pure Term Life Insurance: Choosing Cover & Tenure

If anyone depends on your income—or if you hold a **home loan**—a **pure term life insurance policy** is the single most cost-effective financial shield you can own.

## Why Pure Term Beats Endowment / ULIP Policies

- **Pure Term Plan:** Zero maturity payout; 100% of your premium buys pure life risk cover. A healthy 30-year-old non-smoker can often secure **₹1 Crore+ cover** for roughly ₹10,000–₹15,000 per year.
- **Endowment / Money-Back Plans:** Mix insurance with savings, locking you into high premiums for tiny ₹5–₹10 Lakh cover and ~4%–6% historical IRR.
- **Rule:** Keep **insurance and investments strictly separate**.

## How Much Cover Do You Need?

A practical Indian benchmark:

`Target Life Cover = (12× to 15× Annual Income) + Total Outstanding Loans (Home/Car/Personal) + Major Future Goals − Existing Liquid Investments`

- **Policy Tenure:** Cover yourself until your planned **retirement age (60–65)**—avoid overpriced "whole life up to age 99" plans once your dependents are financially independent.
- **Regular Pay vs Limited Pay:** Regular annual pay usually offers better present-value flexibility unless you have an irregular short career window.

## Avoiding Claim Rejection (Critical!)

1. **Disclose everything truthfully** in the proposal form—smoking/tobacco habits, alcohol, pre-existing conditions (BP, diabetes, thyroid), and family medical history.
2. **Insist on medical tests** rather than skipping them.
3. **Married Women's Property (MWP) Act Addendum:** Married men with business liabilities or home loans can endorse their term plan under **Section 6 of the MWP Act** at purchase so claim proceeds go exclusively to wife and children, shielded from creditors.
