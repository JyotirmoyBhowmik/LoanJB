## Full Description
**LoanJB** is a fast, 100% offline-first Indian personal finance, loan amortization, investment, and income tax planner built for clarity, accuracy, and complete privacy.

### Why LoanJB?
- **100% On-Device Privacy:** Zero accounts, zero analytics SDKs, zero ads, and zero cloud tracking. Your loan schedules, income, and tax calculations never leave your phone.
- **Exact Paise-Level Math:** Powered by deterministic `BigDecimal` accounting so every 360-month amortization schedule closes at an exact ₹0.00 balance.
- **Transparent Data & "As Of" Dates:** Every benchmark rate, small-savings interest rate, and income-tax slab comes from versioned JSON configurations (`rates.json`, `schemes.json`, `taxslabs.json`) with source badges, effective dates, and one-tap reset to defaults.

### Comprehensive Loan & EMI Suite
- **All Major Indian Loan Categories:** Home Loan (EBLR/MCLR/Fixed/Hybrid, PMAY subsidy, Tranche Pre-EMI, Overdraft Offset), Car & Two-Wheeler Loan, Personal Loan (Flat vs Reducing comparison), Education Loan (Simple/Compound Moratorium & Section 80E), Gold Loan (EMI, Interest-Only, Bullet & LTV auction risk), Business & Working Capital (Cash Credit, Bill Discounting, LC/Trust Receipt), Loan Against FD/Property/Shares, and Credit Card EMI (with 18% GST on interest).
- **Prepayments & Floating Rate Resets:** Simulate one-time or recurring part-prepayments (Reduce Tenure vs Reduce EMI) and historical/projected interest rate changes.
- **My Loans Tracker & Alerts:** Track active loans, mark EMIs paid, log real prepayments or floating-rate resets with automatic schedule recomputation, get next-EMI notifications, use the home-screen widget, and follow the Indian Loan Closure Checklist (NOC, Lien/Hypothecation Form 35, CIBIL update, title deed checklist).
- **Side-by-Side Comparison & Export:** Compare up to 3 loan scenarios and export branded Indian-formatted PDF reports, CSVs, or Excel schedules.

### Deposits, Mutual Funds, Taxation & Retirement
- **Deposits & Govt Schemes:** Bank FD (Cumulative, Payout, Tax-Saver), RD, PPF, Sukanya Samriddhi (SSY), SCSS, NSC, KVP, POMIS, and Mahila Samman (MSSC).
- **Mutual Funds:** SIP (with annual step-up & inflation adjustment), Lumpsum, SWP, Goal SIP, XIRR for dated cash flows, Capital Gains Tax estimator, and optional AMFI offline-cached NAV lookup.
- **Indian Income Tax & GST:** Old vs New Regime side-by-side comparison with Section 87A rebate, marginal relief, surcharge, 4% cess, monthly TDS estimate, HRA exemption, GST (CGST/SGST/IGST) calculator, and Advance Tax schedule.
- **Retirement & Financial Planners:** Retirement Corpus & Shortfall planner, NPS, EPF/EPS, APY, PM-SYM, PMVVY, Multi-Goal Planner, 50/30/20 Budget & Insurance Rules, and Educational Asset Allocation guide.
- **30+ Offline Guides:** Bundled Markdown guides on EMI, CIBIL score, prepayment math, tax rules, and small-savings schemes.

### Important Disclaimer
LoanJB is an educational and informational financial planning tool only. It is **not** a bank, NBFC, loan provider, or SEBI-registered investment/tax advisor, and it does not offer or facilitate loans.
